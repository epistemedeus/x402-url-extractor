Usage: acp configure complete [options]

Exchange a requestId for tokens and persist them. Returns {status:'pending'}
until the human finishes sign-in.

Options:
  --request-id <requestId>  The requestId returned by `acp configure start`
  --wait                    Block and keep polling until authenticated or
                            timeout (instead of a single check)
  --timeout <seconds>       With --wait, maximum seconds to wait (default 300)
  -h, --help                display help for command
