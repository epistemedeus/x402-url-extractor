## Rights and durability

Free retention uses the existing customer schema/file and bearer contract with
`evidenceClass: free_observed_delivery`, paid validity/payment permission false
and settlement unknown. It does not relax original paid retention's settlement
requirement. Free rights delegate only read/withdrawal. Wrong
task/attempt/body/method, foreign tenant store, expiry and withdrawal are refusals.

The original customer store has buffered writes and no fsync guarantee. After
acknowledgement loss, physical readback reconciles the existing record, returns
no replacement plaintext grant and causes zero automatic mutation retries.
If the row cannot be established, outcome stays `write_outcome_unknown`.
One writer process and two retained generations remain the limit;
cross-process exclusion is unestablished. Root must preserve that limit.
Any durability upgrade needs separate owned receiving against the original
store, rather than silently introducing a second store.

Caller-artifact fsync and settlement325's original fsync/rename guarantee stay
distinct. Withdrawal removes current rights while preserving historical
useful output/authorized reads. Absence is a useful negative; RPC failure and
bare success claims are unverified. Financial conversion, token savings,
customer counts and unknown spend stay unknown; recognized revenue 0.
