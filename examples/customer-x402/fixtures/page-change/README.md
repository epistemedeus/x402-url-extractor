# Page-change fixtures

Fictional `POST /extract/batch` deliveries for the offline recipe. They are
not receipts, credentials, or evidence of customer demand.

`customer-job/` is the no-key quickstart pair. `merchant/` and `w5-corpus/`
cover reorder, duplicates, missing fields, failed rows, and partial batches.
`c1/` is an alternate batch-result shape used only to prove incomparable kinds.
