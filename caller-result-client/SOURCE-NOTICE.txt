samedaydesk-caller-result is a draft consumer for one optional free statement and, in this version, optional recovery of one already authorized paid result.
It is not a hosted acquisition, a wallet, a payment rail, or a checkout.
The sealed 0.1.0 archive is feedback only and does not include recovery.
This archive carries the public capability contract, the HTTP and native MCP helpers, the optional recovery module, and the optional continuation of one delivered result.
It does not carry merchant server source, HMAC keys, journals, or signers.
Private receipts and customer data are not in this export.
